# SCFA T=8192 50k production run at 1B — 2026-05-13

## TL;DR

Trained CHIRON 1B (870M params, L=24 m=2048 nH=16 dH=256) for 50000
steps at T=8192 with `--scfa --scfa-compression-ratio 16
--no-fuse-attn --fuse-attn-reln --lr-decay`.  Run completed cleanly in
**7h 29m wall**, processed **409.6M tokens** (16× the T=512 baseline),
ended at **ema NLL 4.26 / best 3.76**.  No divergence, no terminal
oscillation.  `chiron_1B_T8192.final` is by far the lowest-NLL CHIRON
1B checkpoint to date — ~7.4 nat below the T=512 baseline's `.final`
oscillation peak.

## Config

```
--seq-len 8192 --m 2048 --layers 24 --heads 16 --dhead 256 --vocab 32000
--int8-adam --bf16-weights --bf16-grads --bf16-attn
--grad-clip 0.5 --lr 1e-4 --warmup 500
--lr-decay --lr-decay-min 0.1                    (cosine to 1e-5)
--accum 1 --scfa --scfa-compression-ratio 16
--no-fuse-attn --fuse-attn-reln                  (validated stable @ L=24)
--max-steps 50000 --log-every 500
--save-every 5000 --save-keep-last 5 --save-full
seed=1337  (fresh init)
```

Required a one-line glades-trainer fix (commit `d209ec9`) to skip the
`scratch_P/dP` allocation under `--scfa` — without it, the [nH, T, T]
buffer at T=8192 would have been 8.6 GB and pushed total VRAM over
15.6 GB.  Final usage at T=8192: 12.91 / 15.56 GB.

stayed in the 4.3–5.4 band the entire late phase.

## Recommended config (new flagship default)

```
--seq-len 8192 --m 2048 --layers 24 --heads 16 --dhead 256 --vocab 32000
--int8-adam --bf16-weights --bf16-grads --bf16-attn
--grad-clip 0.5 --lr 1e-4 --warmup 500 --lr-decay --lr-decay-min 0.1
--accum 1 --scfa --scfa-compression-ratio 16
--scfa-bf16-inner --scfa-bf16-outer --bf16-logits
--no-fuse-attn --fuse-attn-reln
--save-every 5000 --save-keep-last 5 --save-full
```

`--scfa-bf16-inner` added 2026-05-14 (ralph-loop iter 1): routes SCFA
inner attention through `chiron_attention_shear_bf16w_tiled` (BF16-TC
+ no weight casts) instead of `chiron_attention_shear_tiled` (FP32-TC
+ 4 BF16→FP32 casts per layer per direction).  Measured **+10.6%
tok/s** at NLL parity (Δ at step 5000 = +0.0088 nat vs control,
~50× under the 5% margin) and zero new VRAM (BF16 scratches overlay
the free `scfa_qpar` buffer during inner-attention calls).  Gated to
no-op unless `--bf16-weights` is also set.  See
`research/LOOP_RESULTS_2026_05_13.md` iter 1.

`--scfa-bf16-outer` added 2026-05-14 (ralph-loop iter 2): routes the
9 SCFA outer projection GEMMs per layer (B^T·q, B·q_compr, B·y_compr
forward + 6 backward counterparts) through `cublasGemmEx` with
`CUBLAS_COMPUTE_32F_FAST_16BF` (BF16-TC compute, FP32 in/out) instead
of `cublasSgemm` with `CUBLAS_TF32_TENSOR_OP_MATH`.  Measured **+7.96%
tok/s** at NLL parity (Δ at step 5000 = −0.0031 nat vs control —
iter-2 is slightly *better*) and zero new VRAM (FP32 in/out, no cast
scratches).  Stacks orthogonally with `--scfa-bf16-inner`; combined
effect from the T=8192 50k baseline (15,200 tok/s) → 18,183 tok/s,
**+19.6% combined** at unchanged NLL/VRAM.  See
`research/LOOP_RESULTS_2026_05_13.md` iter 2.

`--bf16-logits` added 2026-05-14 (ralph-loop iter 3): routes the 3
tied-readout logits-projection GEMMs (forward `q_L · E^T` + backward
`dlogits · E` and `dlogits^T · q_L`) through `cublasGemmEx` with
`CUBLAS_COMPUTE_32F_FAST_16BF`.  At shape `(T=8192, V=32000, m=2048)
= 1.07 TFLOP` each, these are the largest GEMMs in the model.
Measured **+6.61% tok/s** at NLL parity (Δ at step 5000 = +0.0052 nat
vs control, ~55× under the 5% margin) and zero new VRAM.  Stacks
orthogonally with the SCFA flags; combined effect with all three
ralph-loop wins: 15,200 tok/s baseline → **19,385 tok/s**, **+27.5%
combined** at unchanged NLL/VRAM.  See
`research/LOOP_RESULTS_2026_05_13.md` iter 3.

## Open follow-ups

1. **Long-context inference.**  Run `chiron_infer` on `.final` with
   prompts of varying length (512, 4096, 8192) to verify the long-
   context model isn't degenerate at short prompts.
2. **DISTILL-FORWARD teacher.**  `.step35000` → fresh student.
   Paradigm #56 expects 2–5× sample-efficiency win.
3. **T=16384 feasibility.**  At T=16384 with compression-ratio=16,
   k=1024.  Activation memory ~doubles vs T=8192.  Likely OOM on
   16 GB, but `--accum 2 --seq-len 16384` (half the per-call activations,
   serialized) may fit.
4. **Spike forensics.**  The 11 spikes between steps 13k–48k all
   recovered, but their roots are unclear.  Worth a future analysis:
   are they correlated with specific data shards, or is it intrinsic
   to the SCFA + reln-fuse + bf16-grads gradient path?
