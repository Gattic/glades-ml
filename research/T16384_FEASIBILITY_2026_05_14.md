# T=16384 feasibility test — 2026-05-14

## TL;DR

**T=16384 does NOT fit on 16 GB VRAM** with the current FP32 logits storage. The practical context-length ceiling on the RTX 4080 SUPER (15.56 GB) with the iter-1+2+3+5+9 stack is **T=10240** at ratio=20 (k=512), running at **22,827 tok/s** — actually 9% FASTER than T=8192's 20,947 tok/s because SCFA's compressed-length attention scales sub-linearly with T.

## What was tested

Stack used throughout: `--scfa --no-fuse-attn --fuse-attn-reln
--scfa-bf16-inner --scfa-bf16-outer --bf16-logits --scfa-fuse-streams
--scfa-reln-opt --bf16-weights --bf16-grads --bf16-attn --int8-adam`.

| T     | ratio | k    | --accum | Result | VRAM           |
|------:|------:|-----:|--------:|--------|---------------:|
| 16384 |    16 | 1024 |       1 | OOM    | (alloc fail)   |
| 16384 |    32 |  512 |       1 | OOM    | (alloc fail)   |
| 16384 |    32 |  512 |       2 | OOM    | (alloc fail)   |
| 12288 |    24 |  512 |       1 | OOM    | (alloc fail)   |
| **10240** | **20** | **512** | **1** | **PASS** | **15.25 GB (98% used)** |

## Why T=16384 OOMs

The dominant non-SCFA VRAM cost at T=16384 is the **logits buffer triple** (FP32 per current code):
- `s.logits[T, V]` = 16384 × 32000 × 4 = **2 GB**
- `s.dlogits[T, V]` = **2 GB**
- `s.probs[T, V]` = **2 GB**

That's **6 GB just in logits buffers** vs ~3 GB at T=8192. Combined with the T-scaled activation buffers (s.q, s.p, s.dq, s.dp, s.q_tmp, s.p_norm, s.dq_buf, s.dp_scratch, scfa_qpar/qperp/yperp/ypar, sQ/sK/sV/sO) which roughly double, total VRAM at T=16384 is **~18 GB** — over the 15.56 GB hardware limit.

`--accum 2` does NOT help: CHIRON is fully reversible, so peak VRAM per micro-step is independent of accum count.

## T=10240 validation (200 steps, ratio=20, k=512)

- VRAM: **15.25 / 15.56 GB (2.0% free)** — tight but works.
- Sustained tok/s post-warmup: **22,827** (vs T=8192's 20,947 = +9.0%).
- NLL trajectory (200 steps):
  - step 1:   ema 10.5113
  - step 50:  ema 9.7660
  - step 100: ema 9.2094
  - step 150: ema 9.1556
  - step 200: ema 8.7646
- ‖g‖ spike at step 150 (10.058) recovers to 8.930 by step 200 — same data-driven pattern as T=8192.
- No NaN/Inf.

T=10240 is a STABLE longer-context configuration on this hardware. Worth a full 5k bench + 50k production run if longer context is desired.

## Paths to T=16384 (require code changes)

The dominant blocker is FP32 logits storage. Three options to unlock T=16384:

1. **BF16 logits / dlogits / probs storage** — halves the 6 GB to 3 GB; T=16384 then fits in ~15 GB. **Highest ROI.** Requires modifying softmax / loss / dlogits-write kernels to accept BF16 buffers. ~1-2 day implementation. NLL impact: minimal (FP32 accumulator inside softmax+CE preserves precision).

2. **Chunked logits computation** — compute logits in chunks of T/N tokens, materialize only T/N rows at a time. ~5-10× reduction in logits peak memory but adds latency for the chunk loop. Implementation: replace `q · E^T` GEMM with a tiled loop.

3. **Smaller V (output vocab)** — if vocab can be pruned to ~16k (half), all three logits buffers halve too. Architectural change.

## Compression-ratio considerations at long T

At T=10240, `--scfa-compression-ratio 20 → k=512` was used (matching T=8192's k=512). This keeps SCFA scratches at the same size as the T=8192 baseline. The depthwise conv at `--scfa-conv-w 8` was validated for this k value.

For T=16384 with ratio=32 → k=512 (when VRAM permits): iter-4's finding (NLL fails at ratio=32 at T=8192) does NOT necessarily apply — the *frequency band* the conv must absorb is the same (modes 513..T-1 = ratio-32 modes), but the conv halves T·m·w compute. A separate validation would be needed.

## Recommended next-context-config

For the next production run beyond T=8192, **T=10240 with ratio=20** is the validated ceiling on 16 GB hardware. Gives 25% more context AND 9% higher throughput than T=8192 (combined: +37% more tokens per wall second).

If BF16 logits storage is implemented, **T=16384 with ratio=16** (k=1024) becomes feasible and would give 2× context + ~similar tok/s as T=10240 (since SCFA attention compute scales linearly with k = T/ratio).
