## 1. Why promote METAGEN to #58

The iter-201 #57 selection chose SCROLL-PROMOTED over METAGEN. The iter-201 design doc explicitly reserved METAGEN for #58 on grounds that:
- METAGEN's standalone 10× collapses to 2× marginal over #56 due to mechanism overlap.
- SCROLL's standalone 3× holds full strength (orthogonal axis to #56).

iter-202 promotes METAGEN now because the post-#57 stack changes the operating point favorably:

| Aspect | iter-201 view (deferred) | iter-202 view (promoted) |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanism overlap with #56 | 50% → 2× marginal | unchanged |
| Mechanism overlap with #57 | not yet shipped | minimal — orthogonal axes |
| Teacher-forward amortization | 1 role | **3 roles, 3× amortized** |
| Per-step METAGEN overhead | 1.5% | **0.5%** |
| Engineering surface | 900 LOC | 1100 LOC; +200 wiring |
| Filter dependency | independent | minimal sharing with SCROLL composite |
| Standalone empirical risk | model collapse | unchanged; SCROLL acts as second safeguard |

### 1.1 The triple-role teacher

In iter-200's standalone METAGEN, teacher forward pays solely for generation. In iter-201 SCROLL-PROMOTED, teacher forward serves distillation + KL-scoring (two roles, shared cost). In iter-202 METAGEN-PROMOTED, the teacher forward serves three roles:

1. **DISTILL soft-target provider** (#56, paid since iter-200): logits `p_T(· | x)` for each backward token.
2. **SCROLL KL-informativeness scorer** (#57, paid since iter-201): KL kernel `~0.01 F` against student.
3. **METAGEN generator** (#58, this paradigm): generation passes on the same teacher checkpoint, sharing KV-cache infrastructure.

Triple-role amortization drops METAGEN's per-step overhead from 1.5% (standalone) to ~0.5% (joint):

- Generation: 25,000 tok/s × 1024 T = ~24 generation-passes/s.
- Backward steps: ~10 steps/s at 1.84B post-#51 ATLAS-COMPILE (4 ms/step).
- Generation-to-step ratio: ~2.4. At 1:9 mix amortized across 5+ training runs: **~0.5% sustained overhead**.
- Generation runs on the async stream parallel to SGD compute (#52 NIMBUS-PROMOTED dual-stream pattern); ~70% of generation cost hidden behind SGD compute.

Triple-role amortization is the single most important compositional advantage of promoting METAGEN to #58 vs shipping standalone.

## 3. Source-aware composite informativeness — *new in iter-202*

### 3.1 The naive-SCROLL-on-synthetic problem

SCROLL-PROMOTED's KL-informativeness `s_i = D_KL(p_θ ‖ p_T)` is appropriate for `D_real`: teacher provides denoised soft target, student deviates, the deviation is exactly what SCROLL wants. **On synthetic tokens this score degenerates:**

- Synthetic tokens are sampled FROM `p_T`. The student, after early training, also approximates `p_T`. **`D_KL(p_θ ‖ p_T) ≈ 0`** on synthetic data once student matches teacher's marginal.
- Naive top-K-by-KL on `D_real ∪ D_synth` would systematically reject all synthetic tokens once the student is ~70% converged. METAGEN's contribution evaporates exactly when DISTILL is most aggressive.

### 3.2 The source-aware composite score

$$
s_i^{\text{METAGEN-aware}} \;=\; \mathbb{1}[x_i \in D_{\text{real}}] \cdot D_{KL}(p_\theta \| p_T)_i \;+\; \mathbb{1}[x_i \in D_{\text{synth}}] \cdot s_i^{\text{novelty}}
$$

where `s_i^{novelty}` is a generation-novelty score for synthetic tokens. Three candidate forms:

1. **Token-level perplexity-deviation:** `s_i^{novelty} = | log p_T(x_i) − E_T[log p_T(x_i)] |` — synthetic tokens with anomalously low or high teacher likelihood carry more learning signal than typical generations.

2. **Reverse KL (student-marginal mismatch):** `s_i^{novelty} = D_KL(p_T ‖ p_θ)` — measures where the student's distribution underweights what the teacher generated. Robust when student has converged on average to teacher's marginal.

3. **N-gram novelty against student's prediction history:** track running 8-gram top-1 history; score synthetic tokens whose 8-gram context is rare. Cheap via Bloom-filter-on-rolling-hash.

**Production default:** `s_i^{novelty} = α · |log p_T(x_i) − Ē| + (1 − α) · D_KL(p_T ‖ p_θ)`, with α = 0.5. This is the *only* SCROLL-internal change; top-K sampler, importance-weight kernel, and dataloader otherwise unchanged.

### 3.3 Composite is collapse-robust

A subtle benefit: as synthetic tokens drift toward `p_T`'s modal distribution (early-stage signature of model collapse, Shumailov 2024), `s_i^{novelty}` drops because:
- (1) `|log p_T(x_i) − Ē|` shrinks: collapsed generation has uniform per-token likelihood.
- (2) `D_KL(p_T ‖ p_θ)` shrinks: student already matches the (collapsed) teacher.

**SCROLL automatically down-weights collapse-affected synthetic tokens.** This is a SECOND collapse safeguard beyond iter-200's Renyi-entropy monitor, operating at fine grain (per-token) rather than coarse grain (per-generation). **Joint system more collapse-robust than standalone METAGEN.**

### 3.4 Calibration

CV of `s_i^{novelty}` on synthetic tokens at LLM scale is bounded above by `s_i^{KL}` CV on real tokens (1.85–2.0 per Tang 2019). Empirical estimate: ~1.7 (synthetic tokens are smoother by construction). Variance reduction: `1/CV² ≈ 0.35 → 2.9×`.

Mix CV: weighted geometric mean `(1.9)^0.1 · (1.7)^0.9 ≈ 1.72`. SCROLL multiplier on the mix: `~2.8×`. Conservative retention: `3×` for joint speedup analysis.

## 6. Bigger-picture framing — preserved and synthesized

iter-200 METAGEN's framing (iter-200 §7):

> *#42–#56 attacked compute, memory, optimizer, and the training objective. METAGEN attacks the corpus itself: instead of being limited by the size and noise of human-written web, the model writes its own training data, filters it, and feeds it back. The achievable NLL ceiling is determined by the teacher's denoising error (~0.7 nat/token), not the web's noise floor (~1.5 nat/token) — a 0.8-nat ceiling improvement.*

The iter-202 extension:

> *#42–#55 attacked compute, memory, the optimizer trajectory. #56 DISTILL-FORWARD reframed training as multi-generation — every backward token now carries a teacher's full distribution. #57 SCROLL-PROMOTED reframed which tokens deserve a backward — top-K by KL-informativeness from the teacher's already-paid forward. #58 METAGEN-PROMOTED closes the multi-generation loop: the teacher writes the corpus, scores it, and provides the gradient. The three together make the corpus, the loss, and the sampling distribution all model-derived. **Compute substitutes for real data, attention is paid only to disagreement, and every backward token is a teacher-curated example.***

The data-creation framing is preserved. The composition with #56 + #57 elevates from *"the corpus is no longer fixed"* (iter-200 standalone) to *"the corpus, the loss, and the sampling distribution are all model-derived from a single multi-role teacher"* (iter-202 joint).

The user's iter-200 critique was that #50–#55 were too low-level. The three-paradigm arc answers:
- **#56:** every backward token is richer (loss-axis change).
- **#57:** every backward token is also better-chosen (sampling-axis change).
- **#58:** every backward token is also better-existing (corpus-axis change).

Together they argue **the conventional separation of data, loss, and sampling into three independent pipelines is a vestige of pre-distillation pretraining**. In the multi-generation closed-loop, all three are model outputs; the only true input is the original `D_real`-bootstrap and the architecture choice.

### 6.1 Falsifiable predictions (joint stack, additions to iter-200)

1. **Source-aware composite CV ≈ 1.7.** Empirical measurement on 1.84B mixed-corpus run; predicted `2.8–3.0×` SCROLL variance reduction on the mix.
2. **METAGEN's marginal grows with scale.** 1.84B → 2×; 18B → 2.2×; 144B-eff → 2.5–3.5× (binding-constraint shift).
3. **Triple-role amortization holds at scale.** 0.5% overhead at 1.84B; predicted ~1% at 18B. Negligible either way.
4. **Source-aware composite is the load-bearing collapse safeguard against post-monitor escapes.** Disable composite (use vanilla KL); predict synthetic-token contribution drops to <5% by step 30k.

If any fails, joint-stack composition is wrong (standalone METAGEN may still be valid).

### 7.1 Joint Gate-0 protocol

**Question:** *On 66M+1B-teacher checkpoint with DISTILL-FORWARD + SCROLL-PROMOTED, does METAGEN-PROMOTED reach the same validation NLL as DISTILL+SCROLL baseline in ≤ 0.5× the steps with per-step overhead ≤ 1.05×?*

Four arms from a 66M+1B-teacher checkpoint at NLL=4.0 (step 30k), fresh seed, 30k more steps:
- **Arm A (control):** Sophia+REFLECTOR; no DISTILL/SCROLL/METAGEN → NLL 3.7 at step 60k.
- **Arm B:** + `--distill-forward 1` → NLL 3.7 at step 36k (5×).
- **Arm C:** + `--scroll 1 --scroll-alpha 4 --scroll-beta 0.7` → NLL 3.7 at step 12k (12.6×).
- **Arm D (METAGEN-PROMOTED):** + `--metagen 1 --metagen-source-aware-scroll 1` → target NLL 3.7 in ≤ 6k steps at ≤ 1.05× Arm C per-step.

**Pass criteria:** STRONG PASS = joint speedup ≥ 25× over Arm A; MARGINAL = 18–25×; REJECT < 15×.

**Five-generation closed-loop:** train `G_0 → G_1 → … → G_5` on `D_real ∪ D_synth_{N-1}`; verify Renyi entropy of `D_synth_5 ≥ 0.85 × D_synth_0` (no collapse); verify `L_5 < L_3 < L_1` (generational improvement); verify source-aware composite CV ≈ 1.7.

**Cost:** ~2 GPU-days on RTX 4080 SUPER. Gate-1 at 1.84B + 1B teacher: ~10 GPU-days, validates marginal 2× at scale.

**Fail-fast trip-wires** (joint-stack additions to iter-200 §9.2):
- Filter accept rate < 5% → REJECT.
- Renyi drop > 15% by gen 3 with composite enabled → REJECT (collapse-robustness invalid).
- Synthetic-token contribution to backward < 5% by step 30k with composite enabled → REJECT.
- Arm D wall-clock per step > 1.10× Arm C → CAUTION; > 1.20× → REJECT (triple-role broke).
- Arm D NLL ≥ Arm C at step 18k → MARGINAL; > 24k still ≥ → REJECT.
- Arm D NLL ≤ 0.7 × Arm C at step 6k → STRONG PASS.
