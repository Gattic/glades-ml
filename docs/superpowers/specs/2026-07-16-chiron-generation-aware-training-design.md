# CHIRON ARREST — Generation-Aware Training Against Free-Running Repetition Attractors

**Date:** 2026-07-16

**Status:** Design only; no training run is authorized by this document

**Scope:** a separate, default-off post-training program for a fully trained **prefix-causal** CHIRON checkpoint

**Selected formulation:** **ARREST — Attractor-Responsive Rollout Exposure Stabilization Training**

**Companion plan:** `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-16-chiron-generation-aware-training.md`

> **Operational boundary.** This design does not alter, pause, or consume the active causal FineWeb
> 275k run. The first empirical gate waits for a suitable checkpoint from that lineage. No result from
> the legacy global-DCT checkpoint can by itself authorize generation-aware fine-tuning of the causal
> model.

---

## 1. Executive summary

CHIRON's completed 2026-06-27 inference investigation established a real failure mode in the then-
flagship lineage: free generation fell into low-entropy token and phrase cycles under sliding and
non-sliding windows, greedy and nucleus decoding, numeric and prose contexts, and decoding-side
repetition controls. It also established that simple lexical diversity was an inadequate success
metric: templated loops can have high distinct-4 while remaining plainly degenerate.

That evidence is necessary but no longer sufficient. The diagnosed checkpoint used the legacy
global-DCT SCFA operator, later proved noncausal. Prefix-causal SCFA landed on 2026-07-15. Therefore
the first gate in this program is a **transfer gate**: reproduce and quantify repetition collapse on a
fully trained causal checkpoint. If the causal model does not collapse, this program stops; the old
failure was lineage-specific and no training intervention is justified.

If the failure transfers, ARREST attacks the object inference actually visits: the occupancy measure
of **free-running prefixes**. It alternates:

1. detached rollout collection from fixed real-text prefixes under the current checkpoint;
2. deterministic, prefix-online detection of early repetition risk and collapse-threshold crossing;
3. compact construction of the small set of next tokens that continue the detected pattern; and
4. sparse auxiliary training steps that reduce probability mass on those hazardous actions while
   ordinary clean next-token cross-entropy remains the anchor.

For a free-running state `s` and a deduplicated hazardous token set `H(s)`, define

```text
q_theta(s) = sum_{v in H(s)} pi_theta(v | s)
L_ARREST(s) = -w(s) log(1 - q_theta(s)).
```

The loss is evaluated only on prefixes actually generated by a recent policy snapshot. It is not a
teacher-forced copy tax and is not a retune of ECHO. ECHO penalized excess copy probability on clean
teacher-forced text and failed both quality and mechanism gates. ARREST instead penalizes the
probability flux that keeps an observed free-running trajectory inside a detected attractor.

The minimal implementation reuses the current BF16 readout path and ECHO's proven dense-plus-sparse
kernel pattern, but not ECHO's objective, activation rule, margins, or teacher-forced states. Rollout
collection is out of process and buffer-based, using ratio-preserving reduced SCFA geometry only after
full-geometry parity. Auxiliary backward passes replay collected prefixes with PIED/training-only fields
off, reuse existing trainer scratch sequentially, add no checkpoint or serving-format state, and run at
a sparse cadence targeted to `<=1.5%` amortized training overhead.

A production decision requires all of the following: causal transfer of the failure, a calibrated
non-gameable detector, coefficient-zero parity, direct reduction in free-running hazard mass, at least
three matched seeds, bounded causal validation NLL/top-1, no new instability, large reduction in
collapse incidence under both raw and production decoding, corpus-realism guards, and blinded local-
coherence evaluation. Lexical diversity alone can never pass a gate.

## 2. Evidence and claim boundary

### 2.1 Completed inference evidence that carries forward

`research/CHIRON_GENERATION_LOOP_FIX_2026_06_27.md` established:

- **Window policy was not sufficient:** both full-window slide and grow-without-slide degenerated.
- **Decoding was not sufficient:** greedy and top-k/top-p sampling degenerated; frequency/presence
  penalties and no-repeat n-grams broke exact loops but produced subword/markup soup.
- **The effect was not confined to structured data:** clean prose seeds collapsed into repeated token
  or phrase templates.
- **The attractor strengthened along rollout:** repeated-token logits rose rather than self-correcting.
- **`distinct4` is insufficient:** templated repetition can retain high distinct-4.

These observations justify a rollout-aware metric suite and rule out another inference-only penalty
sweep as the primary program.

### 2.2 Evidence that must not be overclaimed

The diagnosed checkpoint used global-DCT SCFA. That operator compressed each full sequence with
`B^T q`, so teacher-forced states contained future-token information. The causal replacement landed in:

- `glades-ml@142d8802e430e208d38cabad08807b00203ac0e4`;
- `glades-trainer@7b339636e8e09888a502778cfed9255004445b26`.

Consequences:

1. the old teacher-forced NLL and top-1 cannot certify a causal model's quality;
2. the old free-running collapse may have been amplified or caused by the train/inference causality
   mismatch;
3. generation-aware training is conditional on Gate G0b reproducing collapse on the causal lineage.

### 2.3 ECHO closure boundary

The current hard/Huber ECHO objective is closed no-go:

- corrected matched rerun: `7.4741` versus baseline `7.4435` (`+0.0306`);
- activation and copy mass rose rather than fell;
- p99/max-gradient bars failed;
- attribution showed ordinary CE+Z transients, not a dominant direct ECHO field.

ARREST is materially different:

| Property | ECHO | ARREST |
|---|---|---|
| State distribution | clean teacher-forced prefixes | recent free-running prefixes |
| Negative object | over-margin probability on prior clean tokens | tokens that continue a detected rollout attractor |
| Trigger | local frequency/margin hinge | first-hitting/cycle detector on generated trajectory |
| Outer loop | static training distribution | iterative rollout data aggregation |
| Primary claim | reduce copy pressure generally | reduce probability flux into observed collapse states |
| Ship metric | copy mass + NLL | collapse incidence + causal NLL + realism/coherence |

The implementation may reuse ECHO's validated sparse-owner and component-gradient infrastructure, but
must not reuse its objective or interpret ECHO tuning as an ARREST experiment.

## 3. Formal problem statement

Let:

- `V` be the vocabulary and `V*` the set of finite token sequences;
- `mu_train` be the frozen distribution of rollout-training prefixes drawn only from the training
  corpus split;
- `mu_eval` be a disjoint frozen distribution of held-out evaluation prefixes;
- `pi_theta(v | s)` be the prefix-causal CHIRON next-token distribution at state
  `s = (x, y_<t)`;
- `g in G` be a decoding policy; the gate set includes raw greedy, raw nucleus, and the production
  sampler;
- `K_theta^g` be the Markov kernel over generated-prefix states induced by `pi_theta` and `g`;
- `d_theta,t^g` be the occupancy measure of states at rollout step `t`;
- `H` be the rollout horizon, initially `256` tokens.

A detector `D` maps a generated trajectory to a first-hitting time

```text
tau_D(y_1:H) in {1, ..., H} union {infinity}.
```

The causal generation-recovery objective is the constrained problem

```text
min_theta  R_D(theta)
subject to NLL_val(theta) - NLL_val(theta_0) <= epsilon_nll,
           Top1_val(theta_0) - Top1_val(theta) <= epsilon_acc,
           p99_grad(theta) / p99_grad(control) <= 1.15,
           VRAM(theta) <= 15.56 GiB,
```

where

```text
R_D(theta) = E_{x~mu_eval, g~G, y~K_theta^g}[ 1{tau_D(y) <= H} ]
```

is collapse incidence. `theta_0` is the causal checkpoint at fine-tune start. Directly differentiating
`R_D` through discrete rollout sampling is unnecessary and high variance. ARREST uses an alternating,
policy-lagged occupancy surrogate described below.

The program does **not** claim that eliminating repetition proves semantic coherence. It jointly gates
collapse, causal held-out likelihood, generated-distribution realism, and blinded local coherence.

## 4. Observable suite and collapse set

### 4.1 Fixed evaluation corpus

Before Gate G0a, freeze three mutually disjoint artifacts:

1. a detector-calibration set containing synthetic loops, legacy baseline generations, and real held-out
   continuations;
2. a rollout-training manifest drawn only from the training corpus split; and
3. an evaluation manifest of `128` held-out contexts: 64 prose, 24 code, 16 mathematics/markup, and 24
   mixed web text.

The evaluation manifest is stratified into 96 reduced-length no-slide contexts and 32 full-length
no-slide contexts. Sixteen of the full-length contexts also receive a sliding-window diagnostic. Each
entry records corpus SHA-256, document identity, token offset, active length, prompt length, decoded-tail
hash, and split. Calibration/evaluation rows are never admitted to rollout training.

Full-`T` autoregressive collection is expensive because current serving recomputes the whole prefix on
every token. Causal block SCFA permits a cheaper candidate only when block geometry is preserved. For
checkpoint `(T_0,k_0)`, choose `(T_r,k_r)` satisfying

```text
T_r / k_r = T_0 / k_0,  T_0 mod k_0 = T_r mod k_r = 0,
T_r >= prompt_length + H,
```

keep the checkpoint's exact QK-norm gamma and WhiSC state, and prove reduced/full prefix-logit parity
before using reduced rollouts. For the current `T_0=16384,k_0=1024` geometry, the initial candidate is
`T_r=2048,k_r=128`. Changing `T` without changing `k` proportionally is invalid because it changes SCFA
block boundaries. If parity fails, use full `T` and apply the collection-cost kill gate.

Each context produces `H=256` tokens under:

1. **raw greedy:** temperature `0`, all repetition penalties and n-gram bans off;
2. **raw nucleus:** temperature `.8`, top-k `40`, top-p `.95`, penalties/bans off;
3. **production:** current `ChironGenParams` defaults.

Stochastic modes use three fixed decoder seeds per reduced context and one fixed seed per expensive
full-length context; intervals cluster by context. Raw modes measure the model, while production mode
measures the user-facing stack. Collapse seen only after sliding routes to a context-shift diagnosis,
not ARREST.

### 4.2 Metrics

For generated tokens `y_1:H` and periods `p=1,...,64`, let
`R_p=min(H,max(32,2p))`. Report:

- `distinct_n`: unique n-grams divided by total n-grams for `n in {1,2,4}`;
- `max_run`: longest consecutive run of one token;
- `repeat_fraction`: fraction of positions whose token occurred in the prior 64 positions;
- `cycle_p(t) = (1/R_p) sum_{i=t-R_p+1}^t 1[y_i = y_{i-p}]` where indices exist;
- `cycle_max = max_{p,t} cycle_p(t)` and its maximizing period;
- `tau_cycle`: first `t` for which one period has `cycle_p(t) >= .80` with support
  `>=max(32,2p)`;
- `repeated_span_coverage`: fraction of a trailing 128-token window covered by exact repeated spans of
  length at least 8;
- `longest_suffix_copy`: longest current suffix matching an earlier substring;
- `entropy_t`: entropy of the **raw pre-decoding** next-token distribution;
- `margin_t`: raw top-1 minus top-2 logit;
- `entropy_slope` and `margin_slope`: robust slopes over the 32 tokens before detector onset;
- `JS_1`, `JS_2`: Jensen-Shannon divergence of generated unigram/bigram distributions from matched
  real continuations;
- `self_nll`: sampled-token NLL under the raw model, reported for stochastic decoding only.

The provisional collapse event is

```text
C(y) := (max_run >= 8)
        OR (cycle_max >= .80 with support >= max(32,2p))
        OR (repeated_span_coverage >= .60 over a 128-token window)
        OR (distinct_1 <= .15 AND distinct_4 <= .35 over a 64-token window).
```

Gate G0a calibrates these thresholds only on the disjoint detector-calibration set. After G0a,
detector code/config hashes and thresholds are frozen before causal transfer or any treatment run.

### 4.3 Anti-gaming guards

A model does not recover by replacing a loop with high-entropy gibberish. Every recovery table also
reports:

- causal held-out NLL and top-1 on the ordinary validation corpus;
- NLL on the clean positive continuations paired with the generation seed manifest;
- `JS_1` and `JS_2` against those continuations;
- raw entropy and top-1 margin distributions;
- punctuation/control/EOS/pad-token rates and realized generation length versus matched control;
- blind paired local-coherence and gibberish judgments on a frozen sample.

`JS_1/JS_2` are aggregated by domain before comparison; sparse per-sample bigram JS is diagnostic only.
Acceptance is paired noninferiority to the matched control under frozen margins, not an unrealistic
requirement that generated text exactly match one real continuation. `self_nll` is diagnostic and cannot
pass coherence. Final human evaluation uses at least two independent raters, randomized arm order, ties,
inter-rater agreement, and paired bootstrap confidence intervals.

No single lexical metric can authorize advancement.

### 4.4 Statistical contract

- Treatment/control generation is paired by prompt, decoder configuration, and decoder seed.
- Confidence intervals resample contexts as clusters; multiple samples or tokens from one context are
  not treated as independent observations.
- Raw greedy and raw nucleus are co-primary model endpoints; simultaneous intervals use Holm correction.
- Domain results are mandatory secondary endpoints with multiplicity-adjusted intervals; they cannot be
  pooled away to hide a regression.
- The three training seeds are reported individually and as paired seed-level effects; thousands of
  token rows do not inflate the effective seed count.
- Detector thresholds, noninferiority margins, bootstrap procedure, and missing/early-stop handling are
  preregistered in the analysis script hash before treatment.

## 5. Three candidate formulations

### 5.1 Candidate A — ARREST: occupancy-risk control by rollout-conditioned unlikelihood

**Primitive object.** The policy-induced occupancy measure over generated-prefix states and a compact
hazardous next-token set `A(s)` that continues a detected cycle.

**State space.** `S = V*` for prefixes, augmented offline with detector state
`z=(run, ngram table, cycle scores, tau_D)`.

**Evolution.** Generate under a frozen snapshot `theta_r`, construct a rollout buffer `B_r`, then update
`theta` on clean CE plus sparse hazard loss before refreshing the buffer.

**Objective.** For `q_theta(s)=sum_{v in A(s)} pi_theta(v|s)`, minimize
`-w(s) log(1-q_theta(s))` on actual rollout states.

**Strengths.** Directly targets observed attractor occupancy; one current-model backward; compact
hazard lists; no frozen model on GPU; natural DAgger-style refresh; direct component attribution.

**Primary risks.** Detector false positives can suppress legitimate repetition; stale buffers create
off-policy bias; lexical suppression can be gamed by gibberish. Clean CE, confidence weights, frequent
small refreshes, and anti-gibberish gates address but do not prove these away.

### 5.2 Candidate B — PAIR: prefix-anchored iterative ranking

**Primitive object.** For each real prefix `x`, pair the real held-out continuation `y+` with a current-
policy rollout `y-` and compare sequence log-likelihood ratios relative to frozen `theta_0`.

**State space.** Preference triples `(x,y+,y-)` plus cached reference log-probabilities.

**Objective.** A reference-anchored pair loss:

```text
Delta_theta = [log pi_theta(y+|x)-log pi_0(y+|x)]
              -[log pi_theta(y-|x)-log pi_0(y-|x)]
L_PAIR = -log sigma(beta Delta_theta).
```

**Strengths.** Positive real continuations are an explicit anti-gibberish anchor; the loss is sequence-
level and not tied to any repetition detector; it can learn broader coherence preferences.

**Weaknesses.** At 16 GB, positive and negative current-policy gradients must be evaluated sequentially,
while reference scores must be cached. A correct pair update costs multiple full forwards/backwards.
Sequence ranking can increase the real/rollout likelihood gap without changing first-hitting collapse.
It also inherits dataset preference assumptions: the single observed real continuation is not the only
valid continuation.

**Verdict.** Reserve as escalation if ARREST reduces hazard mass but generates incoherent alternatives.

### 5.3 Candidate C — COAST: coherent occupancy alignment of state trajectories

**Primitive object.** Low-dimensional observables of CHIRON's terminal `(q_L,p_L)`, raw logit entropy,
margin, and depth/phase diagnostics under clean and free-running trajectories.

**State space.** Probability measures over detached trajectory summaries, e.g.
`nu_clean(phi)` and `nu_free(phi)`.

**Objective.** Minimize a distribution discrepancy such as sliced Wasserstein or MMD:

```text
L_COAST = D(nu_free(theta_snapshot), nu_clean(theta))
```

with free-running summaries collected offline and clean summaries differentiated through the current
model.

**Strengths.** Nonlexical; directly addresses the dynamical-attractor hypothesis; composes with VITALS
phase-space telemetry; could identify collapse before tokens repeat.

**Weaknesses.** Matching a finite summary is not identifiable: gibberish and coherent text can share the
same entropy/phase statistics. Differentiating only the clean arm may not move the actual free-running
occupancy in the desired direction; differentiating both arms is expensive and sampling-biased. New
summary storage and calibration greatly expand the research surface.

**Verdict.** Diagnostic follow-on, not the minimal intervention.

### 5.4 Selection table

| Criterion | ARREST | PAIR | COAST |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Acts on actual generated states | strong | strong | strong but summarized |
| Direct collapse-control signal | strong | indirect | indirect |
| Anti-gibberish structure | gated | strongest | weak/ambiguous |
| Single-GPU implementation | simplest | expensive sequential pair | medium-high complexity |
| Reuses validated infrastructure | high | medium | medium via VITALS |
| Mechanism attribution | direct hazard mass | preference margin | summary discrepancy |
| Main failure | detector gaming | compute/preference ambiguity | non-identifiability |
| Selection | **SELECT** | reserve escalation | diagnostic reserve |

ARREST is selected because it is the cheapest decisive test of the load-bearing hypothesis: collapse is
maintained by high probability flux through a small, observable set of cycle-continuing actions on
states the policy actually visits.

## 6. ARREST mathematical framework

### 6.1 Hazard set

For rollout state `s_t=(x,y_<t)`, construct a deduplicated set `A(s_t) subset V` from the strict
prefix `y_<t` only. Appending future generated tokens must not change any already emitted hazard row.

1. **run continuation:** the current token if it has already repeated consecutively at least twice;
2. **period continuation:** for each period `p<=64` with confidence at least `.70` over the available
   trailing support, the token that would continue that period;
3. **n-gram closure:** tokens that would complete an n-gram (`n=2,...,8`) already observed at least
   twice in the trailing 128 generated tokens.

The set is capped at `K_A=16` tokens, retaining highest-confidence entries. The cap is a computational
and safety boundary; overflow is reported, never silently truncated without telemetry.

`A(s)=empty` before sufficient evidence exists. Ordinary repetition in real text is therefore not
penalized merely because a token appeared previously.

### 6.2 Prefix-risk and post-onset weights

Let `tau` be detector onset and `c_t in [0,1]` the strictly prefix-online risk confidence. Use

```text
w_t = c_t                         when t < tau and c_t >= .70,
w_t = c_t exp(-(t-tau)/h)         when t >= tau,  h=32,
w_t = 0                           otherwise.
```

This gives causal credit to early run/period evidence before the collapse threshold is crossed and
decays redundant deep-loop rows. It does **not** assign hindsight labels to earlier states from future
tokens. Accordingly ARREST directly controls detected cycle continuation and threshold crossing; it
does not prove control of an unobservable pre-attractor entry action.

### 6.3 Aggregate unlikelihood objective

Define hazard mass from the raw pre-decoding model distribution, never from logits after repetition
penalties, n-gram bans, temperature, top-k, or top-p:

```text
q_t(theta) = sum_{v in A(s_t)} p_theta_raw(v|s_t),   0 <= q_t < 1.
```

For a rollout buffer sampled under snapshot `theta_r`,

```text
L_haz(theta; B_r)
  = (1/Z_r) sum_{(s_t,A_t,w_t) in B_r} -w_t log(1-q_t(theta)),
Z_r = sum w_t.
```

The clean anchored objective is

```text
L_total(theta; B_r) = L_CE(theta; D_clean) + lambda_r L_haz(theta; B_r).
```

No gradient flows through rollout sampling, detector decisions, or `theta_r`. Each row stores behavior
hazard mass `q_{theta_r}(s)`, sampled-action log-probability, and collector snapshot identity. The bias is
explicit: this is a policy-lagged occupancy surrogate, not an unbiased gradient of `R_D`. With `epsilon_s=1e-6`, define staleness as
`|log((q_theta+epsilon_s)/(q_theta_r+epsilon_s))|>log 2`. If more than 10% of active rows are stale,
training pauses for buffer refresh rather than applying importance weights with uncontrolled trajectory
variance.

### 6.4 Exact logit gradient

Let `p_j=pi_theta(j|s)` and `q=sum_{j in A}p_j`. For one weighted row,

```text
dL_haz/dz_j = w p_j (1{j in A}-q)/(1-q).
```

Hence:

```text
j in A:     dL/dz_j =  w p_j,
j not in A: dL/dz_j = -w p_j q/(1-q).
```

The field sums to zero across the vocabulary, so it is invariant to adding a constant to all logits.
It can be implemented as one dense probability-scaled field plus a sparse correction over hazard IDs,
matching the validated ECHO implementation pattern while representing a different objective.

### 6.5 Local monotonicity

**Proposition 1 (derived).** For fixed `s` and fixed nonempty `A`, an infinitesimal gradient-descent step
on `-log(1-q_theta(s))` decreases `q_theta(s)` unless the logit gradient is zero.

**Reason.** The loss is strictly increasing in `q`, and gradient descent changes the loss at first order
by `-eta ||grad_z L||^2`. Since `dL/dq=1/(1-q)>0`, the first-order change in `q` is negative whenever
the gradient is nonzero.

This is a local, fixed-state result. It does not prove that refreshed free-running trajectories avoid all
collapse states.

### 6.6 Alternating occupancy update

At outer round `r`:

```text
B_r <- Rollout(theta_r, M_train, G_train, H)
theta_{r+1} <- Train(theta_r, D_clean, B_r, K clean optimizer steps)
```

The rollout buffer is aggregated with a short horizon of prior rounds, but old records receive geometric
age weight `rho_age^age` (`rho_age=.5`) and records older than two rounds are discarded. This balances
coverage against off-policy staleness.

The candidate schedule is five rounds of 500 optimizer steps (2,500 total), with one auxiliary rollout
micro-step every 25 optimizer steps. At `accum=4`, this is one additional micro-step per 100 ordinary
micro-steps, targeting about 1% amortized training wall before collection cost. It is not accepted until
a measured collector benchmark determines the number and context-length mix of records per round.

Before the first update, a no-update gradient probe on rollout-training records chooses `lambda` from
the fixed grid `{0.003,0.01,0.03,0.1}` so the ARREST parameter-gradient norm is 2–5% of the matched
clean-CE norm. Choose the smallest value in range; if none is in range, G2 fails rather than extending
the grid. The chosen value is frozen for E2/E3; failed efficacy is not followed by an eval-driven
coefficient sweep.

### 6.7 Limiting cases

- `lambda=0` or an empty buffer recovers the causal PIED recipe bit-for-bit.
- A fixed clean-text buffer would reduce toward teacher-forced unlikelihood and is explicitly prohibited.
- A one-token hazard set recovers standard token unlikelihood on an actual generated prefix.
- Replacing the hazard objective with a real-vs-rollout pair loss yields PAIR.
- Matching only detached trajectory summaries yields COAST.

## 7. Algorithm

```text
Inputs:
  causal checkpoint theta_0
  disjoint M_cal, M_train, and M_eval
  decoder set G = {raw-greedy, raw-nucleus, production}
  horizon H, detector D, hazard cap K_A

G0a:
  freeze detector thresholds/config hash on M_cal
  prove reduced-collector versus full-training replay parity and benchmark collection
G0b:
  evaluate theta_0 on M_eval under every g in G
  if collapse does not transfer or affordable replay parity fails: STOP, NO-GO

for outer round r = 0,...,R-1:
  collect detached rollouts from theta_r on M_train
  compute metrics, onset tau, hazard sets A_t, confidence weights w_t
  validate split, source-policy, decoder, geometry, and detector provenance

  for k = 1,...,K:
    execute ordinary causal PIED clean optimizer step
    if global_step mod arrest_every == 0:
      load one rollout record
      replay it in trainer eval-mode (PIED and other training-only fields off)
      verify policy-lag guard, then backpropagate L_haz only
      accumulate normalized auxiliary gradient before the same Adam update boundary

  save isolated candidate checkpoint theta_{r+1}
  evaluate on M_eval; abort on NLL, gradient, collapse, or realism kill gates

return only a research checkpoint; production promotion is a separate decision
```

Rollout generation is deterministic for a manifest/checkpoint/decoder seed triple. At generation step
`t`, the hazard row is constructed before drawing `y_t` and is attached to the same logits row that
produced `y_t`; this alignment is frozen in the buffer and tested with sentinel sequences. Buffer
headers pin checkpoint SHA-256, causal checkpoint flags, tokenizer hash, corpus hash, detector version,
SCFA `(T,k)` geometry, decoder table, and split identity. A separate atomic run sidecar pins buffer hash,
round-start checkpoint, optimizer step range, and output lineage so a mid-round resume can validate
provenance without changing checkpoint or serving formats.

## 8. Theoretical analysis and certainty labels

### 8.1 Hard/derived claims

1. **Logit-shift invariance:** the ARREST gradient sums to zero.
2. **Coefficient-zero isolation:** if implementation does not enter the ARREST branch at `lambda=0`,
   the shipped training path is unchanged.
3. **No model/checkpoint-format state:** detector/buffer/sidecar data are training inputs, not
   parameters or serving state.
4. **Reversibility preserved:** ARREST changes only the terminal loss field; CHIRON's layer maps and
   inverse walk are unchanged.
5. **Fixed-state local effect:** one sufficiently small gradient step reduces hazard mass at the fixed
   rollout state.

### 8.2 Claims under assumptions

1. If the detector's hazard set contains the actions sustaining an attractor and buffer policy lag is
   bounded, repeated alternating updates should reduce detected cycle continuation and collapse-threshold
   crossing; reduction of an earlier unobservable entry action is not established.
2. If clean CE supplies enough restoring curvature, sparse ARREST updates can preserve held-out NLL
   while moving free-running occupancy.
3. If detector false positives are bounded on real text, legitimate repetition should remain intact.

These claims require empirical gates; they are not theorems about global autoregressive dynamics.

### 8.3 Conjectures

- **C1 — sparse-action attractor conjecture:** CHIRON's repetition collapse is maintained by a small
  hazardous token set (`|A(s)|<=16`) at most onset states.
- **C2 — occupancy transfer conjecture:** reducing hazard mass on recent rollout states reduces collapse
  incidence on disjoint prefixes and decoding seeds.
- **C3 — anchor sufficiency conjecture:** ordinary clean CE plus sparse cadence prevents diversity gaming
  without a sequence-level positive preference loss.
- **C4 — causal-lineage transfer conjecture:** a fully trained causal CHIRON still exhibits the old
  repetition attractor. Gate G0b may refute this and terminate the program.

## 9. Computational and systems constraints

### 9.1 Memory

The auxiliary micro-step reuses current `[T,V]` BF16 probabilities/dlogits and model scratch. New device
state is limited to:

- hazard IDs `[T,K_A]` (`int32`, at most 1 MiB for `T=16384,K_A=16`);
- per-row counts/mask/weight and hazard mass (well below 1 MiB);
- compact telemetry.

Target incremental VRAM is `<=8 MiB`; hard ceiling is `<=50 MiB`. There is no second model and no
second simultaneous sequence scratch.

### 9.2 Throughput

- Kernel-only overhead on an active auxiliary micro-step: `<=1%` relative to a normal micro-step.
- Auxiliary cadence target: one extra micro-step per 100 ordinary micro-steps (`<=1.5%` amortized wall,
  including loader overhead).
- Rollout collection GPU-hours are reported separately: `<=10%` of fine-tune GPU-hours is the target
  and `25%` is the hard kill.

The collector must download only one logits row (`V` values) per generated token, not the full `[T,V]`
array currently downloaded by the generic generation loop. It uses the existing device-to-host copy and
stream/event primitives with an explicit compute-to-transfer dependency; a generic `GpuBuffer` API
change is unnecessary. Reduced `(T_r,k_r)` collection is allowed only after same-row logits, sampled
tokens, and behavior hazard mass match full-geometry replay within the G1 tolerance.

### 9.3 Determinism and privacy

Rollout buffers contain token IDs and corpus offsets, not decoded raw documents. They remain local,
carry hashes rather than full source text, and are never written to durable memory systems. RNG streams
reuse the pinned `ChironMt19937` contract. Auxiliary replay forces serving-equivalent PIED-off/eval
mode. The trainer's SCFA activation stochastic-rounding calls currently use static call counters, so an
extra replay would otherwise shift later clean draws and confound the treatment. Replay therefore uses
a separate hash domain derived from `(optimizer_step,record_index,layer,op)` while the existing clean
counter path remains untouched. Coefficient-zero and treatment clean micro-steps must retain the
baseline PIED and stochastic-rounding streams.

## 10. Comparison to existing approaches

- **Scheduled sampling:** mixes model tokens into clean sequences but assigns a ground-truth next token
  after the generated prefix has semantically diverged. ARREST avoids this target-alignment ambiguity by
  applying only a hazard-mass loss on the generated state.
- **Standard unlikelihood:** usually constructs negatives on teacher-forced text. ARREST's defining
  object is the recent policy occupancy measure and first-hitting hazard set.
- **DAgger:** ARREST inherits iterative state-distribution aggregation but has no external expert action
  at generated states. Its supervision is a constrained anti-attractor action set plus clean CE.
- **DPO/preference training:** PAIR is the explicit sequence-preference alternative. ARREST is cheaper
  and tests a narrower causal hypothesis first.
- **Professor forcing/trajectory matching:** COAST is the corresponding state-law alternative. ARREST
  avoids a discriminator and non-identifiable hidden-state matching.
- **ECHO:** ECHO is teacher-forced copy-mass regularization and is closed no-go. ARREST does not treat
  all copy as pathological and never activates without a generated-state hazard record.

## 11. Failure modes and mitigations

1. **The causal model does not collapse.** G0b stops the program; do not manufacture a treatment effect.
2. **Detector mistakes legitimate refrains/code for collapse.** Calibrate on disjoint data by domain,
   cap false positives, use confidence/onset weighting, and report domain-stratified recovery.
3. **Diversity gaming/gibberish.** Require clean NLL, paired corpus-distribution noninferiority,
   entropy/margin guards, and blinded coherence; escalate to PAIR rather than loosening thresholds.
4. **Long or novel cycles evade the detector.** Search periods through 64, report repeated-span coverage,
   inspect residual failures, and version any detector expansion before treatment.
5. **Off-policy buffer staleness.** Store behavior hazard mass, enforce the ratio guard, decay old
   records, and refresh instead of applying high-variance trajectory importance weights.
6. **Serving/trainer replay mismatch.** PIED or other training-only fields would make an auxiliary state
   cease to be the collected free-running state. Force eval-mode replay and require row-logit/hazard-mass
   parity before any update. Isolate replay stochastic-rounding keys so extra calls cannot shift later
   clean-path randomness.
7. **Reduced geometry is not equivalent.** Preserve `T/k` and exact QK/WhiSC state, then prove parity;
   otherwise use full geometry or stop on collection cost.
8. **Gradient arithmetic near `q->1`.** BF16 probabilities need not sum exactly to one. Accumulate `q`
   in FP32, clamp only for finite arithmetic, count every clamp, and kill if clamp frequency exceeds the
   preregistered numerical tolerance.
9. **Legitimate repetition suppression.** Evaluate code/markup separately and include real-continuation
   false-positive and likelihood gates.
10. **New attractors replace old ones.** Fresh rollouts, periods through 64, repeated spans, entropy, and
    blind judgments must pass; detector-targeted hazard mass alone is not recovery.
11. **Termination gaming.** Treatment cannot pass by emitting EOS/pad/control tokens or ending early;
    generation length and termination/control rates are paired noninferiority endpoints.
12. **Optimizer/LR confounding.** Matched arms restore identical optimizer state and use the same
    continuation schedule/data order; only the auxiliary field differs.
13. **Selection or multiplicity bias.** Pair by context/decoder seed, cluster bootstrap by context,
    report simultaneous decoder/domain intervals, and reserve new contexts for G5.
14. **Collection cost dominates.** Use row-only transfer and parity-proven reduced geometry; never shrink
    the manifest below its confidence requirement merely to pass the cost bar.
15. **Current 275k run contamination.** All work uses isolated outputs and later checkpoints; never alter
    the active run's command, data stream, or save prefix.

## 12. Minimal prototype

The minimum meaningful prototype consists of:

1. robust pure-C++ repetition/cycle metrics and a disjoint detector-calibration set;
2. a row-only-logits collector with reduced/full geometry and iterative/replay parity tests;
3. a versioned local rollout-buffer format carrying behavior-policy and provenance fields;
4. Gates G0a/G0b/G1 on a fully trained causal checkpoint;
5. only after those gates pass, CPU and CUDA aggregate-unlikelihood kernels;
6. one default-off, serving-equivalent auxiliary replay cadence and component-gradient probe;
7. a 500-step single-seed matched pilot.

It does not include PAIR, hidden-state matching, online generation inside the trainer, new checkpoint
sections, or production default changes.

## 13. Recovery gate ladder

### G0a — detector validity (disjoint calibration)

- all synthetic constant, period-2/3/8/16/32/64, templated-loop, varied-soup, and real-continuation
  fixtures pass;
- on blindly labeled calibration data with at least 128 positive loops and 64 real-continuation
  negatives per domain: one-sided 95% lower bound on sensitivity `>=.95`, upper bound on overall
  false-positive rate `<=.05`, and upper bound `<=.08` in every domain stratum;
- paired noninferiority margins for aggregate `JS_1`, `JS_2`, raw entropy, top-1 margin, and
  punctuation/control rate are frozen before treatment;
- detector output is prefix-append invariant and deterministic; code/config/data hashes are frozen.

### G0b — causal transfer gate

Evaluate the fully trained causal checkpoint on the frozen 128-context stratified manifest under three
decoder modes for 256 tokens. Use paired context seeds, context-clustered bootstrap, and simultaneous
intervals across the two raw decoder endpoints.

- **GO:** reduced no-slide incidence `>=20%` in at least one raw mode, multiplicity-adjusted 95% lower
  bound `>10%`, and blind review confirms actual repetition/cycle collapse.
- **NO-GO:** reduced and full no-slide incidence `<=5%` in every mode with adjusted upper bounds `<10%`.
- **LONG-ONLY:** full-length no-slide collapses but the reduced stratum does not; proceed only if the
  measured full-geometry training-buffer cost passes G1.
- **SLIDE-ONLY:** only the sliding probe collapses; stop ARREST and open a context-shift diagnosis.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** expand once with new held-out contexts without changing thresholds.

A periodic checkpoint may test instrumentation, but only the fully trained causal checkpoint can close
G0b.

### G1 — collection and replay feasibility

- row-only transfer preserves logits, generated tokens, and RNG draw count;
- ratio-preserving reduced `(T_r,k_r)` and full `(T_0,k_0)` geometry match raw probabilities/hazard
  mass on scored prefix rows within `2e-3` absolute and preserve golden sampled tokens; if not, only full
  geometry is admissible;
- iterative serving and one-pass trainer replay with PIED/training-only fields off have p95 per-row
  hazard-mass difference `<=max(2e-3, .05*q_serving)` before any update;
- collector source checkpoint, split, decoder, geometry, behavior mass, and run-sidecar provenance all
  validate, including mid-round resume;
- measured collection cost is `<=10%` target and `<=25%` hard kill of fine-tune GPU-hours.

### G2 — mathematical and engineering correctness

- CPU finite difference and CPU/GPU parity: relative/absolute error `<=2e-3`;
- row gradient sum `<=1e-6` FP32 reference and BF16-appropriate parity bound;
- loss/gradient normalization is invariant to active-row count, ordinary accumulation, and buffer mix;
- empty hazard set gives exact zero; coefficient zero is bit-identical to baseline;
- duplicate hazard IDs do not double-count; BF16 `q` clamp rate stays below `.1%` of active rows;
- one small gradient step reduces fixed-state hazard mass;
- incremental VRAM `<=8 MiB` target (`50 MiB` hard kill);
- active-kernel overhead `<=1%`; no checkpoint or serving delta;
- full `chiron-generate`, focused ARREST, full `chiron`, checkpoint, and trainer smoke suites pass.

### G3 — 500-step mechanism pilot

Fresh matched control/treatment from the same causal checkpoint and data order.

- newly generated hazard mass falls `>=20%` treatment versus paired control;
- collapse incidence falls `>=50%` relatively on the frozen reduced-length eval stratum and does not
  worsen on the full-length stratum;
- validation NLL delta `<=+0.02`; top-1 loss `<=0.5` percentage point;
- zero non-finite values/skips; p99 gradient ratio `<=1.15`;
- aggregate `JS_1`, `JS_2`, punctuation/control rate, and blinded gibberish meet frozen paired
  noninferiority margins.

Failure closes the minimal objective; do not tune the detector on eval outputs.

### G4 — 2,500-step, three-seed recovery gate

Seeds `1337`, `2024`, and `4242`, each with a fresh same-binary control.

- mean validation NLL delta `<=0.00`, paired 95% upper bound `<=+0.01`, and no seed worse than `+0.02`;
- collapse incidence reduced `>=80%` relatively and absolute incidence `<=10%` in raw greedy and raw
  nucleus on the reduced stratum; any full-length stratum with baseline incidence `>=20%` must meet the
  same relative bar, and all other full/production strata must be non-worse;
- p95 `max_run<=4`, p95 `cycle_max<=.35`, and repeated-span coverage meets frozen paired
  control/real-calibration noninferiority margins;
- clean-continuation NLL delta `<=+0.02` and top-1 loss `<=0.5` point;
- no domain stratum regresses collapse incidence by more than 5 points;
- aggregate `JS_1/JS_2`, entropy/margin, punctuation/control/EOS rates, and realized length meet frozen
  paired noninferiority margins;
- zero skips/non-finites; p99 gradient ratio `<=1.15`;
- amortized training overhead `<=1.5%`; rollout collection is `<=10%` target and `<=25%` hard kill;
- component attribution directly shows the ARREST field lowers hazard mass rather than merely shifting
  later CE transients.

### G5 — final generalization and promotion gate

On 256 new disjoint contexts and at least 64 blind paired judgments:

- context-clustered, multiplicity-adjusted 95% upper bound on collapse incidence `<10%` in both raw
  modes, including the full-length deployment stratum;
- with at least two blinded raters and randomized order, treatment-minus-control coherence preference is
  at least 30 percentage points and its paired-bootstrap 95% lower bound is `>0`; gibberish rate is
  noninferior to control under the frozen margin;
- wide causal validation confirms the G4 NLL/top-1 bars;
- serving/checkpoint parity remains unchanged when ARREST is absent;
- result is documented as a **generation checkpoint**, not silently promoted over the perplexity
  flagship.

Any miss is FAIL or PARTIAL, never a rewritten pass.

## 14. Full research program

If the minimal ARREST objective passes G4 but misses semantic coherence:

1. add PAIR as a separately preregistered escalation using cached reference log-probabilities;
2. use COAST/VITALS summaries only to localize which phase/logit observables precede residual collapse;
3. test longer horizons (`512`, `1024`) and context/domain transfer;
4. study whether first-hitting hazard control admits a Lyapunov bound on collapse probability;
5. replace hand-built hazard sets with a calibrated causal risk model only after the deterministic
   detector establishes a reproducible baseline.

These are not bundled into the minimal implementation, preventing an uninterpretable multi-objective
stack.

## 15. Open conjectures and decision

- **C1:** attractor continuation is sparse in token space.
- **C2:** policy-lagged hazard-mass descent transfers to fresh rollouts.
- **C3:** clean CE is a sufficient anti-gibberish anchor at sparse cadence.
- **C4:** the repetition failure survives prefix-causal retraining.

**Decision:** implement metrics, disjoint calibration, collection/replay parity, and the G0b transfer
harness first. Implement ARREST training only if G0a/G0b/G1 pass. Do not reopen ECHO, alter the active
275k run, or claim generation recovery from diversity metrics alone.
